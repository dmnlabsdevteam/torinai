  consistency = abs(approval_rate - 0.5) * 2 * 0.4
  confidence = sample_confidence + consistency

  sample_confidence = 0.20 * 0.6 = 0.12
  consistency = 0.5 * 0.4 = 0.20
  total confidence = 0.32

For 20 decisions with 90% approval:
